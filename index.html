Max McCormick

Composer for games and film

I’m a composer based in Berlin, working across games, film, and electronic music.

With a background in electronic engineering, my work often sits between music and systems.

I recently contributed an adaptive score to Yerba Buena (Mad About Pandas), a puzzle-platforming adventure set in 1970s San Francisco.

I was privileged to take part in ECHOES, a charity release for the children of Gaza.

Some words written about my music:

“Slow-building grandeur with plenty of sudden left turns” — The Onion
“Beautiful, haunting… an incredible journey of sound” — KALX Berkeley
